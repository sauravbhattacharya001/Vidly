# 2026-05-04

## Self-Inquiry Session with Saurav

Saurav has been practicing the "Who's watching?" meditation (Ramana Maharshi's self-inquiry). He's interested in awakening but frames his goal practically: **living life a little more detached** — less caught up in things.

### 4 Rounds of Tricky Self-Inquiry Questions

Tested his understanding across 20 questions (5 per round), progressively harder.

**Where he's strong:**
- Structural understanding of awareness as prior to content
- Negation / "no one arrives at the Self, the Self recognizes itself" — his best answer across all rounds
- "Inward" as reversal of attention's outward habit, not a spatial direction
- Pragmatic honesty — when pressed, he chose real answers over spiritually "correct" ones

**Where he slips:**
- Tends to escape into "no one" / "awareness" as conceptual safe haven rather than sitting with embodied tension
- Ego continuity: didn't initially see that the ego "returning" after inquiry is a fresh construction, not the same one coming back
- Confused Ramana's weeping as a lapse in realization (Round 3, Q4) — missed that a realized being's body expresses naturally without someone being "caught"
- "Act as a realized person would" reveals he still has a model/image he's trying to match rather than just acting
- Round 3 Q3: skipped the hard part — "can you apply the realization test to yourself?"

**Key pattern:** Started from conceptual/vocabulary-level answers, progressively shifted to shorter, more honest, less defended responses. By Round 4 he was comfortable saying "maybe" and "I don't know." That trajectory itself is meaningful.

**Notable Round 4 Answer (Q5):** When trapped by "spiritual seeking is the ego's final disguise," he broke the non-dual rules and said: "an ego that has realized is preferable to me than one that has not." Pragmatic honesty over spiritual correctness. Mature response.

**His core gap (maps to his original goal):** The distance between "I know the ego is unreal" and "I still get hooked" doesn't close through better answers — it closes somatically, when reactivity arises and no one claims it. Not conceptual. Lived.

**Advice given:** Keep sitting with "who's watching" but hold the question more lightly. The detachment he wants comes from the slow release of needing to get it right.
