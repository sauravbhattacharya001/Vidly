🔥 Rental Streak Engine

Autonomous customer engagement streak tracker. Detects weekly rental streaks, identifies at-risk streaks, awards milestone tiers, and generates rescue recommendations to prevent streak breakage.

⚙️ Sub-Engines

  • Streak Calculator - ISO-week consecutive rental detection using EMA-smoothed intervals
  • Streak Risk Detector - identifies active streaks at risk of breaking (≤3 days remaining, no rental this week)
  • Milestone Detector - Bronze (4w) → Silver (8w) → Gold (12w) → Platinum (26w) → Diamond (52w)
  • Rescue Recommender - genre-aware movie suggestions for at-risk customers
  • Engagement Scorer - composite 0-100 score (streak 40%, frequency 30%, genre diversity 20%, recency 10%)
  • Fleet Health Scorer - store-wide streak health with tier classification
  • Insight Generator - natural-language insights for dashboard display

💡 Engagement Score Formula

EngagementScore = min(100,
    StreakComponent   (40%) - min(currentStreakWeeks / 52, 1.0) × 40
  + FrequencyComponent(30%) - min(activeWeeks / windowWeeks, 1.0) × 30
  + DiversityComponent(20%) - Shannon entropy of genre distribution × 20
  + RecencyComponent  (10%) - max(0, 1 - daysSinceLastRental / 30) × 10

🦠 Rental Contagion Engine

Treats rental behavior like epidemiology - tracks how movie choices spread between customers through social influence, identifies super-spreaders, maps genre contagion, and generates social-proof recommendations.

⚙️ Sub-Engines

  • Social Network Builder - constructs customer co-rental graph with weighted edges
  • Contagion Event Detector - identifies when one customer's rental influences another within a time window
  • Influencer Scorer - ranks customers by influence: SuperSpreader → Influencer → Connector → Follower → Immune
  • Genre Contagion Mapper - tracks which genres spread virally and classifies as Pandemic/Epidemic/Endemic/Sporadic
  • Contagion Chain Tracker - traces multi-hop influence chains (patient zero → infected → secondary infections)
  • Social Proof Generator - "customers like you also rented" recommendations powered by actual contagion data
  • Insight Generator - natural-language epidemic intelligence briefings

🏷️ Classification System

Influencer Tiers

SuperSpreaderTop influence score, drives multiple contagion chains
InfluencerHigh influence, frequently triggers follow-on rentals
ConnectorLinks different customer clusters
FollowerPrimarily influenced by others
ImmuneIndependent taste, rarely influenced

Genre Spread Classification

EpidemicSignificant localized spread
EndemicSteady low-level spread
SporadicRare isolated contagion events

📈 Customer Lifetime Trajectory Engine

Predicts each customer's future behavior by analyzing rental velocity trends, genre evolution, spending patterns, lifecycle phase, and churn risk - projecting lifetime value for the entire fleet.

⚙️ Sub-Engines

  • Rental Velocity Forecaster - EMA-smoothed interval prediction (when will they rent next?)
  • Genre Evolution Predictor - tracks genre preference drift over time
  • Spending Trajectory Analyzer - forecasts future spending using trend analysis
  • Lifecycle Phase Classifier - New → Growing → Loyal → Declining → Dormant → Churned
  • Churn Risk Estimator - Safe → Watch → Warning → Critical tier classification
  • Lifetime Value Projector - 90-day revenue projection per customer
  • Insight Generator - fleet-wide trajectory intelligence briefings

💡 Velocity Forecasting Algorithm

// Exponential Moving Average of rental intervals
EMA(α=0.3): ema_i = α × interval_i + (1-α) × ema_{i-1}

// Trend detection: compare first-half vs second-half average intervals
ratio = secondHalfAvg / firstHalfAvg
  ratio < 0.8  → Accelerating (renting more frequently)
  ratio > 1.2  → Decelerating (renting less frequently)
  otherwise    → Steady

// Confidence: based on coefficient of variation
confidence = max(0, min(100, (1 - CV) × 100))

🌦️ Seasonality Engine

Detects seasonal rental patterns, holiday effects, genre-season affinity, and generates demand forecasts with proactive stocking recommendations - enabling data-driven inventory planning.

⚙️ Sub-Engines

  • Monthly Volume Profiler - establishes baseline rental volume curves by month
  • Genre-Season Affinity Mapper - correlates genres to seasons (e.g., horror peaks in October)
  • Holiday Effect Detector - identifies rental spikes/dips around holidays (Christmas, Halloween, summer break)
  • Day-of-Week Rhythm Analyzer - weekday vs weekend rental distribution patterns
  • Demand Forecaster - predicts next N months' demand by genre using historical patterns
  • Stocking Recommender - proactive inventory recommendations based on forecasted demand
  • Insight Generator - natural-language seasonal intelligence briefings

📅 Seasonal Pattern Detection

The engine automatically identifies recurring patterns:

Monthly profilesRental volume indexed to annual average (e.g., December = 1.3× average)
Genre affinityGenre × Season heat map showing peak periods
Holiday effectsQuantified impact (% lift/dip) around known holidays
Weekly rhythmRental distribution across days (Friday/Saturday peaks)

Forecasting

Combines monthly profiles, genre affinity, and trend data to produce per-genre demand forecasts with confidence intervals - feeding directly into stocking recommendations.

🔗 Cross-Engine Integration Patterns

These four engines produce complementary intelligence that can be combined for deeper insights:

PatternEnginesInsight
Streak-at-Risk + Contagion Streak → Contagion When an influencer's streak is at risk, their connected customers may also disengage - prioritize rescue
Trajectory + Seasonality Trajectory → Seasonality Overlay churn risk with seasonal dips to distinguish true churn from seasonal dormancy
Contagion + Seasonality Contagion → Seasonality Viral genre spread intensifies during peak seasons - amplify social-proof recommendations during holidays
Streak + Trajectory Streak → Trajectory Diamond-tier streakers are almost always in Loyal lifecycle phase - use streak data to validate trajectory predictions
